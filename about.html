Our Story.

Our Legacy.

For over two decades, LittleStars has been at the forefront of early childhood and primary education, shaping the minds that will build tomorrow.

A Journey of Excellence

2001

The Foundation

LittleStars was founded by a group of passionate educators with a vision to revolutionize kindergarten education in the city.

2010

Primary Expansion

Opened our state-of-the-art Primary School wing, introducing grades 1 through 5 with a dedicated STEM lab.

2018

Digital Transformation

Implemented smart classrooms and launched our proprietary Parent Portal and School ERP system.

2026

Award of Excellence

Recognized as the region's top primary educational institution for holistic child development.

Our Mission

Empowering Young Minds

To provide a nurturing, inclusive, and stimulating environment where every child feels valued. We strive to instill a lifelong love for learning through innovative teaching methods and compassionate guidance.

Our Vision

Shaping Global Citizens

To be a globally recognized institution that develops intellectually curious, emotionally resilient, and socially responsible leaders of tomorrow.
