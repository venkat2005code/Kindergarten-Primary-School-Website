How to Apply

A simple, transparent 3-step process.

01

Submit Application

Complete the online application form via our portal and pay the non-refundable registration fee. Our admissions team will review your submission within 48 hours.

02

Campus Visit & Interaction

Once shortlisted, you and your child will be invited for a campus tour and a friendly interaction session with our educators to ensure a mutual fit.

03

Enrollment Offer

Successful candidates will receive an official offer letter. Accept the offer by submitting the required documents and first term fees to secure the seat.
