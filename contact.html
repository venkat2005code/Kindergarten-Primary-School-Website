What is the teacher-to-student ratio?

In Kindergarten, our ratio is 1:12 to ensure personalized attention. In Primary grades, the ratio is 1:20.

Do you provide school transportation?

Yes, we offer a comprehensive bus service covering a 15-mile radius around the campus. All buses are equipped with GPS tracking accessible via the Parent Portal.

Are meals provided at school?

We offer an optional hot lunch program prepared daily by our in-house nutritionists. Vegetarian and allergy-friendly options are always available.
